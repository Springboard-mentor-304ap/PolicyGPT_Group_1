# PolicyGPT

**PolicyGPT – Government Policy & Public Scheme Intelligence Platform**

PolicyGPT is a full-stack web application that helps users discover government policies and schemes, compare policies, check scheme eligibility, and access relevant information through a centralized platform.

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://policygpt-india.vercel.app/](https://policygpt-india.vercel.app/)

## ✨ Features

- **Policy Management** – Search, view, create, edit, approve, and manage government policies.
- **Scheme Management** – Discover schemes, manage scheme details, and track scheme deadlines.
- **Policy & Scheme Comparison** – Compare policies and schemes side by side.
- **Eligibility Checker** – Check eligibility using predefined rules and user information.
- **Authentication & Authorization** – Secure login, registration, OTP verification, and role-based access.
- **Role-Based Dashboards** – Dedicated dashboards for Citizens, Government Officials, Researchers, Organizations, Administrators, and Guest Users.
- **Analytics & Reports** – View statistics, trends, and generate PDF and Excel reports.
- **Notifications** – Receive in-app and email notifications, reminders, and OTP messages.
- **AI Chatbot** – Get assistance with government policies and schemes.
- **Search & Saved Items** – Access global search, search history, and saved policies and schemes.
- **Feedback & Support** – Submit feedback, manage support requests, and access FAQs.
- **User Management** – Manage user accounts, profiles, permissions, and audit logs.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Angular, TypeScript, SCSS, Angular Material, Chart.js
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy
- **Database:** PostgreSQL, Supabase
- **Authentication:** JWT, OTP verification
- **Notifications:** Brevo SMTP, Twilio Verify
- **Deployment:** Vercel (frontend), Render (backend)
